Chapter 1

American Government and Civic Engagement

Overview

Government organizes society, provides public services, protects shared resources, and creates ways to resolve conflicts. This chapter introduces different forms of government, asks who really influences public decisions, and explains how civic engagement connects people to a representative democracy.

Learning goals

01

Explain what government does and distinguish representative democracy from other forms of government.

02

Compare elite and pluralist theories of political power and identify the tradeoffs involved in public decisions.

03

Evaluate how voting and other forms of civic engagement help people influence government.

Goals at a glance

01 · Explain government's roles and forms 02 · Compare theories of political power 03 · Evaluate civic engagement

Why this matters

Government affects your school, roads, safety, taxes, rights, and future opportunities. Knowing how power works — and how to participate — helps you decide whether government is serving the public and what you can do when it is not.
