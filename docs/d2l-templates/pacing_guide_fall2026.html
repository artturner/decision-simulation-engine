Three habits that keep you on pace

1Never let a week's videos roll past that week. The schedule already builds review weeks before FRQs 1–3 — they only work as review if the content is done.
2Watch out for the three "compressed" spots: the Thursday FRQ (Oct 8), the two 3-day weeks (Oct 14 and Nov 4), and the final week (Dec 7). Each one removes your usual slack — get ahead the week before.
3Use the light weeks to bank progress: Sep 21 (no scenario), fall break before Oct 14, and Thanksgiving before the final stretch.
