Chapter 16

Domestic Policy

Overview

Domestic policy is the set of government choices used to address problems within the country. This chapter explains types of policy and goods, major policy areas, the roles of advocates and analysts, the policy cycle, and the budgeting, taxation, and economic decisions that determine who pays and who benefits.

Learning goals

01

Classify public policies and goods by how their costs and benefits are distributed.

02

Analyze major domestic policy arenas and the roles of advocates, analysts, and institutions throughout the policy cycle.

03

Evaluate how budgets, taxes, deficits, and competing economic approaches shape policy choices and outcomes.

Goals at a glance

01 · Classify policies and goods 02 · Analyze policy areas and processes 03 · Evaluate budgets and economic choices

Why this matters

Domestic policy determines what government funds, regulates, protects, or leaves to individuals and markets. It affects health care, education, jobs, retirement, technology, and the taxes you pay, making it essential to ask who benefits, who carries the cost, and whether a policy works.
