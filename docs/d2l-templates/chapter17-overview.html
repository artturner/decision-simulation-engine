Chapter 17

Foreign Policy

Overview

Foreign policy guides how the United States protects its interests and interacts with other countries. This chapter examines major goals and global challenges, tools ranging from diplomacy and aid to sanctions and military force, shared presidential-congressional authority, and competing approaches to international involvement.

Learning goals

01

Explain the major objectives and challenges of U.S. foreign policy in an interconnected world.

02

Compare diplomatic, economic, intelligence, aid, and military instruments and identify when different tools may be used.

03

Analyze how institutions share foreign-policy power and how isolationism, internationalism, hard power, and soft power shape choices.

Goals at a glance

01 · Explain foreign-policy goals and challenges 02 · Compare tools of foreign policy 03 · Analyze power and policy approaches

Why this matters

Events abroad can affect prices, jobs, security, migration, technology, and human rights at home. Understanding foreign policy helps you evaluate claims about war, trade, alliances, and aid — and ask what goals a decision serves, what it costs, and what consequences may follow.
