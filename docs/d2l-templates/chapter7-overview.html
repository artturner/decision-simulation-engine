Chapter 7

Voting and Elections

Overview

Elections connect citizens to government, but participation depends on registration rules, access, motivation, and campaign choices. This chapter covers voter turnout, primaries and general elections, campaign finance, voting behavior, the Electoral College, incumbency, and direct-democracy tools such as initiatives, referendums, and recalls.

Learning goals

01

Analyze how registration rules, voter characteristics, and election policies affect turnout and access to the ballot.

02

Explain how candidates are nominated and elected, including the roles of campaigns, money, parties, and the Electoral College.

03

Evaluate how voters make choices and how direct-democracy tools expand participation while creating new risks.

Goals at a glance

01 · Analyze turnout and ballot access 02 · Explain elections and campaigns 03 · Evaluate voting choices and direct democracy

Why this matters

Soon, if not already, you can vote in elections that shape taxes, schools, courts, and public policy. Knowing the rules and strategies behind elections helps you participate effectively and recognize how access, money, messaging, and turnout can influence the result.
