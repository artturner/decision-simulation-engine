Chapter 13

The Courts

Overview

Courts interpret laws, resolve disputes, protect rights, and review government action. This chapter examines the dual state-federal court system, the structure and selection of federal courts, the Supreme Court's procedures, and the legal, ideological, and political forces that affect judicial decisions and their implementation.

Learning goals

01

Explain judicial review and the structure, jurisdiction, and purposes of state and federal courts.

02

Trace how federal judges are selected and how cases reach, proceed through, and are decided by the Supreme Court.

03

Evaluate the influences on judicial decision-making and the checks and practical limits on judicial power.

Goals at a glance

01 · Explain court structure and review 02 · Trace judges and cases 03 · Evaluate decisions and limits

Why this matters

Court decisions can change the meaning of laws and rights long after an election ends. Understanding the judicial system helps you follow major cases, recognize why judges may disagree, and see why a ruling still depends on other institutions and the public for implementation.
