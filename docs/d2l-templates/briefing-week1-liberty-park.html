American government and civic engagement

A developer wants your neighborhood park, and city hall is listening to whoever bothers to show up. This week is about what showing up actually buys you — who governs, who gets heard, and what a single engaged citizen can and cannot move.

Objectives. After this chapter you will be able to:

  • Compare elitism and pluralism as accounts of who governs
