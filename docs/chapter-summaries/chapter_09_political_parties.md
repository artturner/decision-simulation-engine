# Chapter 9: Political Parties

## Chapter Overview

Political parties organize elections, connect voters and candidates, and help officials govern. This chapter explains how the U.S. party system developed, why election rules favor two major parties, how parties operate at different levels, and how divided government and polarization affect policymaking.

## Learning Goals

1. **Trace** the development of American political parties and explain why the United States has a durable two-party system.
2. **Explain** how parties organize, nominate candidates, mobilize voters, and coordinate officials in government.
3. **Analyze** how party coalitions, divided government, and partisan polarization influence representation and policy outcomes.

## Goals at a Glance

1. **Trace** party development.
2. **Explain** what parties do.
3. **Analyze** division and polarization.

## Why This Matters

Party labels shape ballots, campaigns, lawmaking, and the information people see. Understanding parties helps you look beyond team loyalty, compare what candidates and coalitions actually support, and recognize why government sometimes acts quickly and sometimes stalls.
