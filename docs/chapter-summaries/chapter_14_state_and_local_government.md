# Chapter 14: State and Local Government

## Chapter Overview

State and local governments exercise broad authority and deliver many services people encounter every day. This chapter explores state power and revenue, political culture, governors and legislatures, the debate over term limits, and the structures and responsibilities of county and municipal governments.

## Learning Goals

1. **Explain** how states and localities receive, share, and use power and revenue within the federal system.
2. **Compare** state political cultures, governors, legislatures, and arguments for and against legislative term limits.
3. **Distinguish** major county and city government structures and connect them to the services communities receive.

## Goals at a Glance

1. **Explain** state and local power.
2. **Compare** state politics and institutions.
3. **Distinguish** local structures and services.

## Why This Matters

The government decisions you notice most often may be local: school funding, policing, roads, water, zoning, and parks. Understanding state and local institutions makes it easier to find the right decision-maker and participate where your voice can have an immediate effect.
