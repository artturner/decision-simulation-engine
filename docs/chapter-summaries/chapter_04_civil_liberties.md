# Chapter 4: Civil Liberties

## Chapter Overview

Civil liberties protect individuals from improper government interference. This chapter examines the Bill of Rights, selective incorporation through the Fourteenth Amendment, freedoms of religion and expression, protections for people accused of crimes, and continuing debates over privacy and public safety.

## Learning Goals

1. **Explain** how the Bill of Rights and selective incorporation protect civil liberties from federal and state action.
2. **Apply** constitutional protections involving religion, expression, firearms, searches, due process, and criminal procedure.
3. **Evaluate** how courts balance individual liberty against public order, safety, and competing rights.

## Goals at a Glance

1. **Explain** how liberties are protected.
2. **Apply** key Bill of Rights safeguards.
3. **Evaluate** liberty-safety tradeoffs.

## Why This Matters

Civil liberties shape what you may say, believe, post, carry, and expect from police or courts. Knowing their limits helps you recognize when government may lawfully act, when a right may have been violated, and why hard cases rarely have simple answers.
