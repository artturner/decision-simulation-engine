# Chapter 1: American Government and Civic Engagement

## Chapter Overview

Government organizes society, provides public services, protects shared resources, and creates ways to resolve conflicts. This chapter introduces different forms of government, asks who really influences public decisions, and explains how civic engagement connects people to a representative democracy.

## Learning Goals

1. **Explain** what government does and distinguish representative democracy from other forms of government.
2. **Compare** elite and pluralist theories of political power and identify the tradeoffs involved in public decisions.
3. **Evaluate** how voting and other forms of civic engagement help people influence government.

## Goals at a Glance

1. **Explain** government's roles and forms.
2. **Compare** theories of political power.
3. **Evaluate** civic engagement.

## Why This Matters

Government affects your school, roads, safety, taxes, rights, and future opportunities. Knowing how power works - and how to participate - helps you decide whether government is serving the public and what you can do when it is not.
